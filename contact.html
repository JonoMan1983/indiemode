Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take? +
We deliver via Pargo to your nearest pickup point. Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days from order confirmation.
What is your returns policy? +
14 days, no questions asked. Items must be unworn with original tags. Jewellery is excluded from returns for hygiene reasons.
Can I order internationally? +
Not yet — Indiemode currently ships within South Africa only. International shipping is coming in Q3 2026.
How do I list my brand? +
Fill in the contact form on this page with "List my brand" as the subject. We review all applications within 5 working days.
Are all products really SA made? +
Yes, strictly. We visit every designer studio before approving their listing. No imported or dropshipped product is allowed on the platform.
How do I track my order? +
You'll receive a Pargo tracking link via email as soon as your order is dispatched. You can also track via the Pargo app.
