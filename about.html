The Origin

Built for the makers.

Before Indiemode, South African independent designers sold through Instagram DMs, WhatsApp groups, and pop-up markets. Great craft was invisible to buyers who weren't already in the know.

We built a platform that gives every independent SA designer the same digital shelf space as international brands — with no gatekeeping, no monthly fees to get started, and no minimum stock requirements.

Every designer on Indiemode is hand-curated. We visit studios, check production methods, and verify that every piece is genuinely SA-made before it goes live on the platform.

What We Stand For

Three things we never compromise.

01
Authentically South African

Every piece on Indiemode is designed, cut, and finished in South Africa. We verify this with every designer before listing. No dropshipping. No imported product rebranded as local.

02
Independent by Definition

We only work with owner-operated brands. No chain stores, no franchises. Every brand on our platform is built and run by the person whose name or vision is behind it.

03
Community Over Commission

We take a fair commission — lower than any comparable marketplace — because we believe designers should earn more from their work, not less. Our revenue grows when theirs does.
