    Our Mission

    Why Indie­mode exists

    South Africa has extraordinary fashion talent. Designers cutting garments by hand in Woodstock studios, jewellers casting silver in Cape Town backyards, textile artists weaving in KwaZulu-Natal. Most of them have no way to reach a national audience.

    Indiemode was built to fix that. A single, curated marketplace where South Africans can discover and buy directly from independent designers — without the retail markup, without the middleman, without the BS.

    Every rand you spend here goes directly to a South African maker. That's the deal. That's always been the deal.

    What we stand for

    Three principles.

    01 — Transparency

    No hidden margins

    Designers set their own prices. We take a flat platform fee. You see exactly what the maker earns from every transaction.

    02 — Curation

    Every brand is handpicked

    We don't accept everyone who applies. Our team reviews every designer for craft quality, sustainability practice, and South African origin.

    03 — Community

    Designers first, always

    We run workshops, masterclasses, and pop-up events across SA. Building the ecosystem matters as much as building the marketplace.
